
PROJECT SUBMISSION SUMMARY

Project: F&B Ops Intelligence
Subtitle: AI-Powered F&B Demand, Ordering & Reconciliation System

Objective:
Build an AI-enabled operations platform that transforms client meal requirements into recommended vendor orders, monitors delivery and consumption, detects anomalies, and produces operational recommendations.

AI Components:
- Random Forest demand prediction
- Isolation Forest anomaly detection
- AI-style operational recommendation engine

Technology:
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Streamlit, Plotly, Joblib

Business Use Case:
Reduce manual effort in requirement consolidation, improve demand planning, identify delivery discrepancies, reduce avoidable wastage, and improve vendor performance visibility.

Data:
Synthetic operational dataset created for demonstration; no confidential company data used.
